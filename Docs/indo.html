Training Native-Speaker Linguists

Two of the community members who have worked with me have gone forward in linguistics:

  • Jupri Talib, my primary consultant since 2018, has begun to pursue a Ph.D in linguistics at Hassanudin University
  • Nabila Haruna became the Language Ambassador of South and West Sulawesi in 2021.

Jupri Talib and I have also given joint talks at the Annual Conference at the American Institute for Indonesian Studies at MSU:

  • Subjecthood in Linguistic Theory
  • Linearization and Phonological Displacement

I have also run a series of practical workshops on Using Praat for Language Documentation at:

  • Hasanuddin University
  • the Language Office of South Sulawesi
  • the Language Department of the National Agency for Research and Innovation in Makassar

Collaborating with Local Linguists

With Maria Tamarina Prawati, at Bina Nusantara University (Jakarta), I have worked on various aspects of Mandar poetry. The first stage of this work was conducted with Jupri Talib and presented at the Annual Conference on Linguistics at Atma Jaya University.

With Jusmianti Garing, at the National Agency for Research and Innovation in Makassar, I haves conducted surveys on microvariation in definitness marking across South Sulawesi. The next stages of this work will be presented at Hassanudin University in 2027.

Advocating for Linguistics Departments

I have spoken before several government agencies about language documentation and the role of generative research in that project:

  • At the National Agency for Research and Innovation in Makassar, which allocates funds to universities and government agencies tasked with documentary work in the Mandar region, on [the utility of generative research in documentary work]
  • At the Language Office of East Java on "the role of linguistic research in cross-cultural studies"

On the invitation of Sitti Sapiah, a Mandar language activist and native-speaker linguist, I recently spoke at the main university in the Mandar region, the University of West Sulawesi, to support a petition for the foundation of a local linguistics department .

Engagement with the Mandar Community

In the Mandar region, I have tried to establish a public-facing profile that seems sensible, useful, and generally approachable to people. Since 2019, I have worked with universities and cultural institutions, given speeches at cultural events, and appeared in several venues to speak about bilingualism, language maintenance, documentation, and the general riddle of what my research really is. At one point I appeared on a podcast with Ridwan Alimuddin, a local author and documentary filmmaker, in an episode posted on youtube here.

Engagement with the Linguistics Community

I have given talks and generally been around at four universities since 2018:

  • The State University of Malang (where I gave a lost talk on historical linguistics in 2018)
  • Universitas Hasanuddin (e.g., "Metrical Theory in South Sulawesi")
  • Atma Jaya University (on linearization and suppletion)
  • Bina Nusantara University (on language documentation, subjecthood, traditional poetry, and regional language pedadgogy)

I've also presented at the Annual Linguistics Conference at Atma Jaya (on second-position clitics) and taught individual sessions of graduate and undergraduate classes at Bina Nusantara and Atma Jaya on various aspects of syntactic theory.
