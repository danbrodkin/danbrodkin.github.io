Supporting Linguistics in Indonesia

Beyond conducting fieldwork, I take it as a major long-term goal to support the development of linguistics as a discipline in Indonesia. In Jakarta, I have given class lectures at Atma Jaya University and Bina Nusantara University and invited talks on language death and documentation, ergativity, linearization, and suppletion. In Sulawesi, I have built a similar set of connections with the local departments and government bodies that conduct linguistic research, and I have spoken at the National Agency for Research and Innovation [("What's the Use of Generative Linguistics?")], Universitas Hasanuddin in Makassar ("Metrical Theory in South Sulawesi"), Universitas Sulawesi Barat in the Mandar region. I have also given invited lectures at the Language Office of East Java ["Generative Linguistics and Language Pedagogy: Definiteness and Definte Articles in Sulawesi"] and a series of talks with my primary language consultant, Jupri Talib, at the Annual Conference at the American Institute for Indonesian Studies at MSU (on subjecthood and movement).

Collaboration in Sulawesi

Over my time in graduate school, I ran a series of Praat workshops at the Language Office of South Sulawesi, the government body tasked with language documentation work across the region. In 2023, I also began a collaborative project with one linguist from this office, Jusmianti Garing (Umi), to document and analyze microvariation in the system of definitness marking across the region. During my fulbright year in 2018-2019, I also digitized and glossed the complete descriptive literature on two languages of this area, Mandar and Mamuju, and uploaded this work as a publically accessible and searchable corpus on Kratylos. Since 2018, at last, I have appeared on local podcasts, given talks at local schools, and spoken at a range of cultural events across the Mandar area.
